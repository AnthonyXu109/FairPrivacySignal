## Interpretation Limits

FairPrivacySignal is a synthetic engineering benchmark. It does not model a real community, provide a production privacy guarantee, prove that fairness gaps are solved, or establish that a particular threshold is appropriate for deployment. See [limitations.md](limitations.md) for the full scope statement.
